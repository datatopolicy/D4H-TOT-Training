## Training of Trainers (TOT) — Resource Hub

> **CDC Foundation · Data for Health (D4H) Initiative**  
> Sheraton Istanbul City Center · March 29 – April 2, 2027  
> Bangladesh · Côte d'Ivoire · Rwanda · Tanzania

---

### About This Repository

This repository is the single source of truth for the five-day Training of Trainers workshop. It contains every facilitation resource, downloadable training file, and reference document needed to deliver and reproduce the workshop — organized so that national trainer cadres can access and reuse materials independently after the initial TOT week.

**Project Goal:** Strengthen the analytical and reporting capacity of government agencies and partners to improve the quality, consistency, and dissemination of public health surveillance and mortality data, including CRVS data where available and relevant.
