## Pre-TOT Deliverables

Due **before Monday, March 29.**

### 1. Identify Sources of Data
A list of every relevant data source your agency has access to for candidate priority topics.

### 2. For Each Data Source, Document:
- Department and institution collecting the data
- Timeliness and quality of the data
- Goals of collecting the data
- Types of reports generated from it
- Data dictionaries (if they already exist)
- The process for accessing the data
- Challenges accessing the data

### 3. Priority Disease/Condition Dataset
A dataset on your selected priority disease or condition covering the **past five years**, broken down by **district** and by **sex**.

> 📊 Use the template: `assets/workbooks/Priority_Disease_5yr_District_Sex_Template.xlsx` — fill in the yellow cells and delete the example row before the training.

> 📝 Sign off using: `assets/templates/Pre_and_Post_TOT_Deliverables_Checklist.docx`
