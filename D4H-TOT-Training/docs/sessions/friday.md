## Group Report Presentations

**9:30–12:30 (and continued 14:00–15:00)**

**Format per team:**
- **12 minutes** — presentation using the standard PowerPoint template
- **6 minutes** — panel Q&A
- Strict time limits, enforced consistently across all four teams

**Panel composition:** Government representatives · CDC Foundation staff · Mentors · Peer teams

**Responding to panel questions:**

1. **Restate** the question briefly to confirm understanding.
2. **Answer** using the finding/interpretation/recommendation structure.
3. If a question cannot be answered from the current data, say so clearly: *"We can't answer that from this data, but it would be a valuable next analysis."*

> ⚠️ Honest acknowledgment of data limits is a **strength**, not a weakness, in front of a real audience.

**Panel feedback form:** Three strengths + two areas to strengthen per team. Compiled and shared same day.

**Standard PowerPoint presentation template structure:**

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Title: topic, country, team |
| 2 | Background & Objectives |
| 3 | Methods (brief) |
| 4–6 | Findings (one headline finding per slide — title states the finding, visual supports it) |
| 7 | Equity Considerations |
| 8 | Recommendations |
| 9 | Thank you / Questions |

## Report Guidelines Wrap-Up & Certification

**15:30–16:00**

Recap the two standards every future cycle will be held to:
1. The standard **eight-section report template**
2. The **finding / interpretation / recommendation** discipline

**Trainer certification:**

| Status | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Full Trainer** | Demonstrated facilitation quality + technical mastery + effective feedback delivery across the week |
| **Co-Facilitator** | Strong technical mastery; facilitation delivery still developing — paired with a Full Trainer for the first cycle |

Certification conversations happen **individually** (5 minutes each) — never announced in the open group session. Every participant leaves with one status and a dated first action.

> 🗂️ **Facilitator tip:** Co-Facilitator status is not a lesser outcome — it is a structured developmental path, and should be framed as such.
